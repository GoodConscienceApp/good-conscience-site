Why it exists

Your phone asks for everything else. Why not your attention?

The camera, the microphone, and location already get a pause. Attention can have one too.

Apps should ask permission for your attention.

You don’t have to quit. Just choose.

Good Conscience is a phone app designed to make you think about whether you want to use another phone app.

  • Your attention is a permission.
  • A second thought, built in.
  • No guilt. Just a choice.
