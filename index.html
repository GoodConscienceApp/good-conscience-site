Attention permission

The permission your phone forgot.

Your phone asks permission for your camera, microphone, and location. Good Conscience makes apps ask for your attention too.

A clear choice. A clear conscience.

How it works

Choose first. Then open.

  1. 01

    An app asks

    It requests access to your attention — the same kind of pause your phone already uses for the camera.

  2. 02

    You choose how long

    5, 10, 15, 30 minutes, or no timer. Fifteen starts selected. Make the next minutes yours.

  3. 03

    Check in with real life

    Before you go in, see what’s waiting — tasks, people, a gratitude, a question, how you’re doing. Right under Allow.

  4. 04

    You allow

    Then you open the app. Scroll if you mean it.

  5. 05

    It asks again

    When the time ends, you get the same clear choice.

A clear choice. A clear conscience.

Features

Before you go in

While an app is waiting for permission, Good Conscience can show you real life first — then you Allow and open it.

  • Task nudges

    Ask someone to do something concrete. When they open a watched app, your ask can show up on that pause screen before they scroll.

  • Open tasks

    Things people asked you to do stay on that same screen under Allow, so what’s waiting isn’t buried somewhere else.

  • Your life

    Your own reminders and lists live there too. One suggestion at a time — you can skip to the next.

  • Gratitudes

    Send a short thanks. It can land for them on that pause before they dive into an app.

  • Questions

    Link with people you choose (not your phone contacts). Ask what matters; their answers can show on the pause screen.

  • Moods

    Share how you’re doing with those people — simple, one update, no feed to manage.

  4. J Jordan · Gratitude 12:01 Thanks for covering dinner
  5. S Sam · Answer 12:05 Yes. I’ll bring the salad.
  6. A Alex · Mood 12:02 😊 Good

Why it exists

Your phone asks for everything else. Why not your attention?

The camera, the microphone, and location already get a pause. Attention can have one too.

Apps should ask permission for your attention.

You don’t have to quit. Just choose.

Before selected apps open, you choose how much attention to give. Nothing is blocked.

  • Your attention is a permission.
