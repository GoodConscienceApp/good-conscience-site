Good Conscience privacy policy

Updated October 29, 2026.

Good Conscience is a nudge app. You put your own life in it. When you open an app you chose to watch, it can offer something from that life before the scroll goes on.

What stays on this phone

Your categories, tasks, reasons, groups, gratitudes, the mood you pick, the people you add, a photo you add for a person, your settings, and the answers you write under How can you show up for them stay on this phone. Answers you write under How can they show up for you are sent to their phone. Items other people have already sent you stay on this phone once it has saved them, including answers they wrote about how you can show up for them. So do notices that a task you sent was accepted, denied, completed, or forgotten, and the counts on Insights. The name and the link for a person you have linked are also saved to your Good Conscience account. A photo you add is not uploaded.

We do not keep a permanent copy of that life in the cloud. Uninstalling the app clears this phone only. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional for your own tasks and gratitudes. A mood does not open until you are signed in and at least one person is linked, because a mood is sent to them. Sign-in is required before someone can link with you or send you anything.

You sign in with the Google account on this phone. We receive the account id Google gives the app, and the name on that account when Google provides one. That account id is what tells accounts apart. This app does not use an email address to tell accounts apart. We do not receive your Google password. We do not see your email inbox, contacts, photos, or files.

Your code is that account id, shown as a QR code. It is not shown until you have read the explanation and this phone has accepted your PIN or screen lock.

Linking

Scanning someone's code saves their account id on your phone, next to the name you gave them. Your phone also saves that name and that account id to your Good Conscience account. It does not save their tasks, notes, or messages.

Signing out does not delete a link and does not make anyone scan again. It marks this phone as not accepting new sends. Signing back in with the same Google account turns that back on.

You can pause a person. New sends from them stop until you turn them back on. Anything already waiting stays, and it stays hidden while they are paused. You can unlink or delete them. That removes the cloud flag and anything still waiting from them, so they cannot send again. It only counts once you are online. If it cannot reach the cloud, they can still send and the app says to try again.

Uninstalling this phone does not remove those links. After you install again and sign in with the same account, the names and the links come back. Tasks, gratitudes, moods, and the answers you wrote on this phone do not come back. If this phone still has an answer someone else wrote, signing in can send that answer back to their phone so their list can fill in again. Deleting the Good Conscience account is different. That removes the names, the links, and the account. The next sign-in is a new code.

Delete the account

You can delete the Good Conscience account from Privacy and data. That removes the names, the cloud links, and the account, then signs you out. The next sign-in is a new code. The old code stops working. This does not delete the Google account. An app update does not do this, and neither does uninstalling. If the app is no longer installed, email goodconscienceapp@gmail.com from the Google account you used, or ask at https://goodconscienceapp.github.io/good-conscience-site/delete.html. The account and the data saved on it are deleted within 7 days. A Play subscription is not canceled by this request. There is no separate way to erase the saved names and links while keeping the same account.

What is sent through Firebase

Firebase, a Google service, is the handoff. It is not a permanent inbox. The connection is encrypted.

When you send a mood, a nudge, a gratitude, a task, an answer about how they can show up for you, or a nudge that asks them to answer a question, Firebase holds that one item until their phone saves it. An answer they wrote about how you can show up for them is sent back the same way. The item has your account id, the name saved for you, the words you sent, a short detail such as a reason or a question number, the kind of item, and the time. Their phone then deletes that cloud copy. You can read an answer they wrote. You cannot change it. One comment can go back with a seen item. It is not a chain.

If they already have 100 unopened items from you, the next one does not send. You are told their inbox for you is full. You are not unlinked.

Accepted, denied, completed, and forgotten notices go through Firebase the same way, then stay on the sender's phone. That list stops at 50. You clear one, or clear all. Leaving the list does not clear it.

Accessibility

Watched-app nudges need Android's accessibility switch. This app uses that switch to read the name of the app in front, such as the name of a watched app, and to know when that app comes forward or leaves. It looks at the window only to read that name. If you choose Sleep, agreeing to leave the app locks the screen. Sleep stays off until you choose it. That choice stays on this phone. If you choose the home screen, or the switch is off, the screen does not lock, and the phone goes to the home screen instead.

This version does not read the words on the screen, your messages, your photos, or your passwords. It does not read what you type. It does not hide the keyboard. It does not take the clipboard. It does not press keys or tap the screen for you. It does not use your location.

You choose the watched apps. The list stays on this phone. The app can see the names of apps that have an icon, so you can pick which ones to watch. It does not read the contents of those apps. The service does not start a nudge for an app that is not on that list. If the switch is off, Good Conscience cannot tell that a watched app opened. The access screen and the timer do not run. Things other people already sent still show when you open the app. You can still open the app and fill in your life. The use it is built for does not work until the switch is on.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan someone else's code. The picture is not saved and is not uploaded.

Subscription

Good Conscience is free for 3 days on this phone. After that, a subscription is required to keep using it. If the 3-day trial is still open on that Google account, you are not charged when you subscribe. Google gives you 3 more free days. When those days end, Google charges the plan you picked: $79.99 a year, which renews every year until you cancel, or $11.99 a month, which renews every month until you cancel. You only get that trial once. If it was already used, Google charges the plan price when you subscribe. We do not receive or store your card number. Cancel in Google Play before those extra days end if you do not want to be charged. Open Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Manage subscription in Settings opens the same page. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. The subscription stays on the Google account, not on this phone.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use your location. We do not post phone notifications for nudges. We do not require a link for your own tasks and gratitudes to work. A mood does not open until you are signed in and someone is linked. We do not share this data with other companies for their own use.

What is not allowed

Do not send threats, hate, sexual content, harassment, or spam. You can pause or unlink a person. That stops new sends from them. On a linked person's page, Report sends what you choose to goodconscienceapp@gmail.com. They are not told. Reports are reviewed, and a link that breaks these rules is removed.

Questions about this policy: goodconscienceapp@gmail.com
