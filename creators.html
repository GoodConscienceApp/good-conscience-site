Creator Opportunities

Say it plainly.

Good Conscience is looking for creators, and for people who can represent the brand in person.

Your phone asks permission for your camera, microphone, and location. Good Conscience makes apps ask for your attention too. That missing moment is the whole idea.

  • What we are asking

    Show the moment of choice. An app asks for your attention. You choose how long. Then you mean it. No lecture. No guilt.

  • Who this is for

    Creators who can explain one clear idea in public. Brand representatives who can say it calmly, in person, to people who would actually use it.

  • How to write

    Email goodconscienceapp@gmail.com with the subject CREATOR OPPORTUNITY. Tell us who you are, where you publish or where you meet people, and why this permission is worth a minute of someone’s attention.
