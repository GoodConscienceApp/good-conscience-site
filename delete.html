Delete your Good Conscience account

Updated October 23, 2026.

This page is for the Good Conscience app. You can ask for the account to be deleted even if the app is no longer on your phone.

Email a deletion request

Send that email from the Google account you used in the app. The request is finished within 7 days.

What is deleted

The Good Conscience account is deleted. The names and the links saved on that account are deleted. Anything still waiting to be delivered is deleted.

What is not deleted

Your Google account is not deleted. A subscription bought through Google Play is not canceled by this request. Cancel that in the Play Store if you do not want to be charged.

Tasks, gratitudes, moods, answers you wrote, photos, and settings stay on the phone. They are not kept in the Good Conscience account. Removing the app from the phone removes them. Answers already saved on someone else's phone stay there.

If the app is still installed

You can also delete the account in the app. Open Good Conscience, go to Settings, then Privacy and data, then Delete Good Conscience account.
